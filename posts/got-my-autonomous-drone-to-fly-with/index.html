Got my autonomous drone to fly with a heavy payload!

Bags of rice for now, but cameras and computers next round

Apr 16, 2026 · Autonomy & Mapping · by Daniel Jordan Viray

New milestone re: my autonomous drone: I got it to fly properly with a heavy payload!

Last week, my drone was struggling to fly because the upgraded computer and camera were heavy. Not only was this causing bad flight, but it was also causing the drone to crash and damage its components. Crashes were fine when the parts were cheap, but now that I'm flying with expensive and limited-supply equipment, I can't afford crashes anymore.

So this last week, I put a lot of effort into 3D printing and hardware engineering. I created custom landing gear, propeller guards, mounting systems, and mounts, all with precise measurements. AND I cut out any unnecessary mass. This did/does two things: (1) distributed the weight properly so that the drone could fly, and (2) protects the equipment as much as possible if/when there are crashes.

And it worked! In this video, the drone is flying well with the dummy computer and camera (i.e., bags of rice) mounted in their proper positions!

Next step is to put the real hardware onto the drone and then get back to the software development.
