First 3D drone simulation using Gazebo

It’s too high-risk and costly to test AI drone code in the real world

Mar 8, 2026 · Autonomy & Mapping · by Daniel Jordan Viray

My guiding vision for my autonomous drone project is to eventually deploy these drones in search and rescue operations.

This week, I learned how to simulate drones in a physics-based 3D virtual world. With the codebase becoming increasingly complex, I realized that it’s too high-risk and costly to test the drone code in the real world.

In this simulation video, my drone can successfully launch on its own, make its way to the rescue site, and then return. My next step is to give the drone the ability to search for the rescue site entirely on its own when the target is initially hidden from the field of view.
